회계TERM 1 ✓

Financial Accounting

세 재무제표가 한 거래로 어떻게 동시에 움직이는지를 손으로 그릴 수 있으면 끝. 이익과 현금은 다르다.

COGS(콕스) · GAAP(갭) · EBITDA(이비따) · Accrual · Depreciation · Retained Earnings · Gross Margin(매출총이익)

통계TERM 2 · NOW

Statistics

공식 암기보다 "이 질문엔 어떤 검정인가"의 의사결정 트리. 종착점은 회귀.

p-value · Standard deviation ≠ Standard error · H₀ "H-naught" · Confidence interval · R-squared · Variance(분산) · Deviation(편차)

미시경제TERM 1 ✓

Microeconomics

그래프를 직접 그리기 — 수요곡선의 이동과 곡선 위의 이동을 구분. 영어 수식의 방어선은 그래프.

Marginal(한계) · Sunk cost · Opportunity cost · Elasticity · WTP · Oligopoly(올리가폴리) · Nash equilibrium · Price discrimination(부정적 뉘앙스 없음)

거시경제TERM 2 · NOW

Macroeconomics

Fed 결정 → 금리 → 환율 → 자기 회사 실적까지 내려오는 인과 사슬 하나를 만들어 두고 수업 내용을 거기에 붙인다.

The Fed · Federal funds rate · Inflation / Deflation / Disinflation · Real vs. Nominal · QE · Yield curve · Currency appreciation / depreciation(회계의 depreciation과 다른 뜻)

ManagementTERM 1 ✓

Strategy · HSC · Global

Strategy는 미시의 "왜"와 회계의 "성적표"를 경쟁우위 논리로 묶고, Global은 거시의 국가 차이를 진입·운영 결정으로, HSC는 그 결정을 사람과 조직으로 실행한다. 세 개가 한 과목인 이유: 결정(전략) 없이 실행(HSC) 없고, 국경(Global) 없는 전략은 없다.

Competitive advantage · Porter's five forces · Value creation vs. value capture · Core competence · Entry mode · Liability of foreignness · Psychological safety · Social capital(인맥이 아니라 관계망의 자원)

LGSTTERM 2 · NOW

Responsibility in Business

다른 과목이 "어떻게 이기나"를 가르치면, 이 과목은 "누구에게, 무엇을 책임지며 이기나"를 묻는다. 법(compliance)은 바닥선이고 윤리·이해관계자 책임은 그 위의 판단. 케이스에서 "합법이지만 옳은가"를 구분하는 게 핵심 훈련.

Stakeholder vs. Shareholder · Fiduciary duty · Compliance ≠ Ethics · Corporate social responsibility (CSR) · ESG · Whistleblowing · Conflict of interest · Duty of care / loyalty
WEMBA 52 · Core Term

EMBA Core Course Map

Macro, Micro and Accounting are the content — how to read the world. Statistics is the method that tests whether the reading holds. Management (Strategy · HSC · Global) is the action layer that turns the reading into decisions and organizations. LGST (Responsibility in Business) is the boundary every one of those decisions has to pass through.

One-line summary and key vocabulary per course

AccountingTERM 1 ✓

Financial Accounting

You are done when you can draw by hand how one transaction moves all three statements at once. Earnings are not cash.

COGS · GAAP · EBITDA · Accrual · Depreciation · Retained earnings · Gross margin · Working capital

StatisticsTERM 2 · NOW

Statistics

Less formula memorization, more of a decision tree: "which test answers this question?" The destination is regression.

p-value · Standard deviation ≠ standard error · H₀ · Confidence interval · R-squared · Variance · Outlier · Correlation ≠ causation

MicroeconomicsTERM 1 ✓

Managerial Economics

Draw the graphs yourself — a shift of the demand curve versus a move along it. When the algebra goes fast, the graph is your defense.

Marginal · Sunk cost · Opportunity cost · Elasticity · Willingness to pay · Oligopoly · Nash equilibrium · Price discrimination (no negative connotation)

MacroeconomicsTERM 2 · NOW

Macroeconomics

Build one causal chain — Fed decision → rates → FX → your own company's results — and hang everything from class on it.

The Fed · Federal funds rate · Inflation / deflation / disinflation · Real vs. nominal · QE · Yield curve · Currency appreciation / depreciation (not the accounting sense)

ManagementTERM 1 ✓

Strategy · HSC · Global

Strategy binds Micro's "why" and Accounting's scorecard into the logic of competitive advantage; Global turns Macro's country differences into entry and operating choices; HSC executes those choices through people and organizations. Why one course: no execution (HSC) without a decision (Strategy), and no strategy without borders (Global).

Competitive advantage · Porter's five forces · Value creation vs. value capture · Core competence · Entry mode · Liability of foreignness · Psychological safety · Social capital (a network's resources, not "networking")

LGSTTERM 2 · NOW

Responsibility in Business

The other courses teach how to win; this one asks whom you answer to, and for what, while winning. Compliance is the floor; ethics and stakeholder responsibility are the judgment above it. The core drill in every case: "legal, but is it right?"

Stakeholder vs. Shareholder · Fiduciary duty · Compliance ≠ Ethics · Corporate social responsibility (CSR) · ESG · Whistleblowing · Conflict of interest · Duty of care / loyalty

各课程一句话总结与关键词汇

会计第1学期 ✓

Financial Accounting

能徒手画出一笔交易如何同时牵动三张报表，就算学到位了。利润不等于现金。

COGS(销货成本) · GAAP · EBITDA · Accrual(权责发生制) · Depreciation(折旧) · Retained earnings(留存收益) · Gross margin(毛利率) · Working capital(营运资金)

统计第2学期 · 当前

Statistics

少背公式，多建一棵决策树："这个问题该用哪种检验？"终点是回归。

p-value · Standard deviation(标准差) ≠ standard error(标准误) · H₀ · Confidence interval(置信区间) · R-squared · Variance(方差) · Outlier(离群值) · Correlation ≠ causation(相关不等于因果)

微观经济第1学期 ✓

Managerial Economics

自己动手画图——分清需求曲线的移动与沿曲线的移动。代数推得太快时，图就是你的防线。

Marginal(边际) · Sunk cost(沉没成本) · Opportunity cost(机会成本) · Elasticity(弹性) · Willingness to pay(支付意愿) · Oligopoly(寡头) · Nash equilibrium(纳什均衡) · Price discrimination(价格歧视，无贬义)

宏观经济第2学期 · 当前

Macroeconomics

搭好一条因果链——Fed 决策 → 利率 → 汇率 → 自己公司的业绩——再把课上的所有内容挂到这条链上。

The Fed(美联储) · Federal funds rate(联邦基金利率) · Inflation / deflation / disinflation(通胀/通缩/通胀放缓) · Real vs. nominal(实际 vs. 名义) · QE · Yield curve(收益率曲线) · Currency appreciation / depreciation(货币升值/贬值，与会计的 depreciation 不同)

Management第1学期 ✓

Strategy · HSC · Global

Strategy 把微观的"为什么"与会计的"成绩单"绑成竞争优势的逻辑；Global 把宏观的国家差异转化为进入与运营选择；HSC 则通过人与组织执行这些选择。三者合为一门课的原因：没有决策（Strategy）就没有执行（HSC），没有国界（Global）就谈不上战略。

Competitive advantage(竞争优势) · Porter's five forces · Value creation vs. value capture(价值创造 vs. 价值获取) · Core competence(核心能力) · Entry mode(进入模式) · Liability of foreignness(外来者劣势) · Psychological safety(心理安全) · Social capital(关系网络中的资源，而非"拉关系")

LGST第2学期 · 当前

Responsibility in Business

其他课程教你如何取胜；这门课追问的是：在取胜的同时，你对谁负责、负什么责。合规（compliance）是底线，伦理与利益相关者责任是底线之上的判断。每个案例的核心训练："合法，但对吗？"

Stakeholder(利益相关者) vs. Shareholder(股东) · Fiduciary duty(受托责任) · Compliance ≠ Ethics(合规不等于伦理) · Corporate social responsibility (CSR) · ESG · Whistleblowing(举报) · Conflict of interest(利益冲突) · Duty of care / loyalty(注意义务/忠实义务)
